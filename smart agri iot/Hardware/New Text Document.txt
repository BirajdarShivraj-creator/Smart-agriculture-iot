Hardware Required
ESP32 Development Board
Main microcontroller
Handles sensor readings, processing, and system control.
Soil Moisture Sensor
Measures moisture content in the soil.
Provides data for monitoring field conditions.
NPK Sensor
Measures Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium levels in the soil.
Helps determine nutrient requirements.
DHT11/DHT22 Temperature & Humidity Sensor
Measures surrounding temperature and humidity.
Rain Sensor
Detects rainfall conditions.
Can be used to prevent unnecessary agricultural operations during rain.
Water/Fertilizer Flow Sensor
Measures the flow rate of liquid fertilizer or nutrient solution.
Useful for monitoring the quantity being supplied.
NPK Fertilizer Hoppers/Containers
Separate storage containers for Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium fertilizers.
DC Pumps / Peristaltic Pumps
Used to transfer and mix liquid nutrients from the respective containers.
Relay Module / MOSFET Driver Module
Provides electrical control of pumps and other high-current loads from the ESP32.
DC Geared Motors
Used for mechanical operation such as opening/closing fertilizer mechanisms or rotating the mixing system.
Motor Driver Module
Controls the direction and speed of DC motors.
LCD/OLED Display
Displays sensor readings and system status locally.
Wi-Fi Connectivity through ESP32
Enables transmission of sensor data to an IoT platform or mobile/web interface.
Power Supply Unit
Suitable regulated supply for ESP32, sensors, pumps, and motors.
Battery/Solar Power System (optional)
Can provide power for outdoor agricultural deployment.
Connecting Wires, Breadboard/PCB, Terminals & Connectors
Required for electrical connections and hardware integration.
Enclosure/Control Panel
Protects the electronic components from dust, moisture, and agricultural environmental conditions.
